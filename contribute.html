Contribute

Help history live on.

Your support helps the museum share the stories of South Central Oklahoma with future generations.

Museum General Fund

Contributions support educational and public programming, exhibits and other museum activities. To give by mail, send a check payable to GSHM to Greater Southwest Historical Museum, 35 Sunset Drive SW, Ardmore, OK 73401.

Memorial gifts

Commemorate someone special with a gift to the museum. Contact our team to discuss your dedication.

Santa’s House on the Prairie

You can give to the Museum General Fund or sponsor a Holiday Forest tree. Tree sponsorship helps support Santa’s House on the Prairie. Your name is displayed on a tree in the Holiday Forest, where children listen to stories and watch the Daube Puppets. You may decorate your tree or have museum staff decorate it for an additional fee. Staff will do their best to use your chosen colors or theme. Contact the curator for current sponsorship prices and display dates.
