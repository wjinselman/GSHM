Santa’s House on the Prairie

Tree sponsorship helps support Santa’s House on the Prairie. Your name is displayed on a tree in the Holiday Forest, where children listen to stories and watch the Daube Puppets. You may decorate your tree or have museum staff decorate it for an additional fee. Staff will do their best to use your chosen colors or theme. Contact the curator for current sponsorship prices and display dates.
