Gainesville, Texas to Ardmore

Visit GSHM from Gainesville, Texas

An Oklahoma history visit from North Texas

Traveling from Gainesville, Texas? Make the Greater Southwest Historical Museum in Ardmore your destination for a look at South Central Oklahoma history. The museum is housed in a former National Guard armory and brings together regional industry, everyday life and military exhibits.

Directions from Gainesville

The museum is at 35 Sunset Drive SW, Ardmore, OK 73401. Open the route below with Gainesville, TX as the starting point, or change the starting address to your home or hotel. Check the map when you travel for current route options and traffic; driving time depends on your starting point and road conditions.

Build your visit around the armory

Start with the limestone building, constructed in 1935–1936 by the Works Progress Administration, then explore the collections inside. An early electric car and the steam-powered fire engine show different sides of the area’s past. Check the museum’s Tuesday–Saturday schedule before making the trip; it is closed Sundays, Mondays and holidays.

Hours and admission

Free admission. Tuesday–Saturday · 10 a.m.–5 p.m.. Closed Sundays, Mondays and holidays. Call 580-226-3857 before traveling for a holiday visit, group program or special appointment. The museum is in Ardmore, not in Gainesville.

Planning for your group

Tell the museum about your group size, interests and any access needs before your trip. Group reservations need at least one week’s notice. Genealogical research has separate hours, and seasonal programs require their own booking arrangements.
