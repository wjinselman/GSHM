Membership

Be part of the story.

Membership helps keep local history alive through exhibits, education and community programs.

Stay connected

All membership levels receive quarterly newsletters and invitations to special events. Some levels include additional rental benefits. Contact the museum to join.

Membership levels

  • Student / Senior — $15: standard benefits
  • Individual — $25: standard benefits
  • Couple — $35: standard benefits for two people
  • Family — $50: standard benefits for all family members, plus a 5% rental discount
  • Business — $100: standard benefits, plus a 10% business discount on museum rentals
  • Sustaining — $100: individual benefits, plus a 10% discount on museum rentals
  • Patron — $250: Sustaining benefits, plus one Education Room rental fee waiver
  • Benefactor — $500: Patron benefits, plus one Noble Hall rental fee waiver

Join or renew

Contact the museum for a membership application and to confirm current benefits, rental terms and availability. Patron includes Sustaining benefits; Benefactor includes Patron benefits.
