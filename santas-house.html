Santa’s House on the Prairie

A little holiday wonder.

Santa’s House on the Prairie is an event designed for schoolchildren ages three to seven years old. Participating children enjoy decorating ornaments, exploring our Holiday Forest, and listening to stories read by Mr. and Mrs. Claus.

A memorable morning

Begin in the Holiday Forest, enjoy stories with Mr. and Mrs. Claus, decorate an ornament and receive cookies. Allow about 30 minutes for the activities.

Plan your group’s visit

The program takes place during the first two weeks of December. Please call between August and October to schedule. Appointments are offered at 15-minute intervals from 9 to 11:30 a.m. The listed cost is $2 per child; confirm current availability and pricing when booking. Spaces fill quickly, so please reserve early.

Help make the magic

Volunteers help keep the program running throughout its two-week season. Roles include movie attendant, playing Mr. or Mrs. Claus, group attendant and crafting assistant. Contact Cassandra, our curator, by email or call the museum to volunteer.

Sponsor a Holiday Forest tree

Tree sponsorship helps offset program costs. Sponsors’ names are displayed on the trees. The existing program lists a display period from the beginning of November through January 1; confirm this season’s dates with the curator. Small and large trees are available. Decorate your own tree or ask staff to decorate it for an additional fee; staff will do their best to use your chosen colors or theme. Contact Cassandra for current prices.
