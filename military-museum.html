Military Memorial Museum

Honoring those who served.

Preserving military history and honoring the people of all service branches who have served this country in war and peace.

History

The Military Memorial Museum is housed in the west wing of the building that once served as a National Guard Armory for Oklahoma’s historic, battle-proven 45th Division. Built by the Works Progress Administration in 1935–1936, the building itself is a monument to that period in Oklahoma’s and the nation’s history. The men and women who serve as guides, or “Charge of Quarters,” are volunteers.

Exhibits

Displays in the museum represent all eras: the Civil War, the Spanish-American War, the Indian Wars, World War I, World War II, the Korean Conflict, Vietnam, the Gulf War and the Iraqi Freedom War. The displays include artifacts, documents, photographs, uniforms, weapons, knives, models, medals and a great deal of personal memorabilia from veterans all over the United States.

Mission

Our mission from the beginning has been to preserve and display military history, and to honor those persons of all service branches who have served this country in war and peace.

Funding

Although the Military Memorial Museum is located in the Greater Southwest Historical Museum, it is self-supporting and does not share in the gifts or donations made to the larger museum. Memberships and Hall of Honor donations are important sources of support.

Hall of Honor

A donation of $50 purchases an engraved plate showing the donor’s name and the name of the recipient being honored. Memorials may be made “In Memory Of” a veteran family member or veteran friend from any state or branch of service. The entrance and main corridor of the Military Memorial Museum are recognized as the Hall of Honor.

Ardmore Army Air Field history

Explore the history of Ardmore Army Air Field (1942–1946) and Ardmore Air Force Base (1953–1959). This memorial website is dedicated to the men and women who served with distinction at Ardmore Army Air Field, a training base for the U.S. Army Air Forces.
