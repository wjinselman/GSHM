A little time. A whole lot of history.

Discover the people, places and everyday objects that tell the story of South Central Oklahoma. Start with a warm welcome in our historic armory.

Come on in

Free admission. Tuesday–Saturday · 10 a.m.–5 p.m.. Closed Sundays, Mondays and holidays. If you plan to visit over a holiday, call before you travel.

Parking, accessibility and visit length

Bus and motorcoach parking is available. The scavenger hunt is wheelchair accessible and takes about 45 minutes to an hour. For help finding an entrance, planning a drop-off or choosing galleries that fit your schedule, call 580-226-3857.

Call about your visit →Call about your visit580-226-3857

Visiting with a group?

Reserve group tours and programs well in advance to help secure your preferred date, with at least one week’s notice for all groups. School groups must bring at least one adult chaperone for every 15 students. Neustadt Park is available for those bringing lunches. Tell us about any access needs so we can help you plan.

Research visits

The Genealogical Library has separate hours: Wednesdays, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., and by special appointment. Please call ahead. The library is closed throughout December.
