Plan your visit

A little time. A whole lot of history.

Discover the people, places and everyday objects that tell the story of South Central Oklahoma. Start with a warm welcome in our historic armory.

Come on in

Admission is free. We are open Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed Sundays, Mondays and holidays.

Find us in Ardmore

35 Sunset Drive SW, Ardmore, OK 73401. Call 580-226-3857 for help planning your visit.

Visiting with a group?

Reserve group tours and programs well in advance to help secure your preferred date, with at least one week’s notice for all groups. School groups must bring at least one adult chaperone for every 15 students. Neustadt Park is available for those bringing lunches. Tell us about any access needs so we can help you plan.
