Sulphur, Oklahoma to Ardmore

Visit GSHM from Sulphur

Discover the stories behind the objects

Add an Ardmore museum visit to your plans from Sulphur. The Greater Southwest Historical Museum introduces the region through photographs, archives and three-dimensional artifacts. The indoor galleries offer a change of pace, with opportunities to explore transportation, home life and the people who served.

Directions from Sulphur

The museum is at 35 Sunset Drive SW, Ardmore, OK 73401. Open the route below with Sulphur, OK as the starting point, or change the starting address to your home or hotel. Check the map when you travel for current route options and traffic; driving time depends on your starting point and road conditions.

Choose your own pace

Begin with the gallery that most interests you, then make time for From the Ashes and Ardmore’s first steam-powered fire engine. The Military Memorial Museum is in the west wing. If your group has particular access needs, call before setting out so museum staff can help you plan.

Hours and admission

Free admission. Tuesday–Saturday · 10 a.m.–5 p.m.. Closed Sundays, Mondays and holidays. Call 580-226-3857 before traveling for a holiday visit, group program or special appointment. The museum is in Ardmore, not in Sulphur.

Planning for your group

Tell the museum about your group size, interests and any access needs before your trip. Group reservations need at least one week’s notice. Genealogical research has separate hours, and seasonal programs require their own booking arrangements.
