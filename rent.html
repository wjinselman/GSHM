A place to come together

Rent the museum

Gather somewhere memorable.

The museum and its grounds may be available for non-commercial, private functions in a setting surrounded by the history of South Central Oklahoma.

Noble Hall

A setting for receptions, business meetings, holiday parties and special events. Noble Hall is available outside the museum’s operating hours.

The Education Room

Located in the north wing, the Education Room suits small meetings, birthday parties, wedding and baby showers, and workshops. It is available during operating hours. A $50 reservation deposit is required. Half-day rental time slots are 10 a.m.–1:30 p.m. or 1:30–5 p.m. Please confirm availability when booking.

Tools of Our Land

Ask about gathering in the Tools of Our Land exhibit. Rentals may be arranged Monday through Saturday.

Plan your event with us

Contact the museum to discuss your date, group size, current rental rates and guidelines, and request the rental brochure and Guidelines for Private Functions. Rates and documents are being reviewed; confirm the applicable fees and requirements before booking.
