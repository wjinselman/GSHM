Step into the past

Explore the museum

Every object has a story.

Follow your curiosity through the galleries, from life on the land to the industries and people that shaped the region.

Transportation & industry

Explore early carriages, saddles, soapbox derby cars and a working model of oil-field equipment. Discover how railroads, cotton and oil helped shape South Central Oklahoma.

From the Ashes

Discover the region’s history and see Ardmore’s first steam-powered fire engine.

Tools of Our Land

Explore the machinery and tools of the region’s farming and ranching heritage.

Keep exploring

Discover military history, family research and something to remember your visit by.
