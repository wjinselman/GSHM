Marietta, Oklahoma to Ardmore

Visit GSHM from Marietta

Bring the generations together

A trip from Marietta can be a chance to share family memories through the objects on display. Early carriages, household items and farming equipment give different generations something concrete to explore together. The museum is in Ardmore; the directions below lead to its Sunset Drive SW address.

Directions from Marietta

The museum is at 35 Sunset Drive SW, Ardmore, OK 73401. Open the route below with Marietta, OK as the starting point, or change the starting address to your home or hotel. Check the map when you travel for current route options and traffic; driving time depends on your starting point and road conditions.

Turn a visit into a conversation

Look for an object that someone in your group remembers using, then compare it with the tools and machines in Tools of Our Land. For family-history questions that need more than an exhibit visit, arrange time with the Genealogical Library and check its separate Wednesday hours.

Hours and admission

Free admission. Tuesday–Saturday · 10 a.m.–5 p.m.. Closed Sundays, Mondays and holidays. Call 580-226-3857 before traveling for a holiday visit, group program or special appointment. The museum is in Ardmore, not in Marietta.

Planning for your group

Tell the museum about your group size, interests and any access needs before your trip. Group reservations need at least one week’s notice. Genealogical research has separate hours, and seasonal programs require their own booking arrangements.
