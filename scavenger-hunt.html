Learning through discovery

Scavenger hunt

Follow a clue. Find a story.

Explore the museum through clues that encourage students to look closely, read exhibit signs and think about life in Oklahoma.

An adventure through history

Groups follow clues to artifacts and gallery areas, discovering stories from territorial days to the present.

Plan your hunt

Allow 45 minutes to one hour. The scavenger hunt is wheelchair accessible. Contact the museum to discuss your group’s needs.
